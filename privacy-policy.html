ClutchSprint — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

ClutchSprint ("we", "us") operates automated tools that generate and publish short sports video clips to Instagram and TikTok on behalf of the @clutchsprint account. This page explains what data our integration touches when it connects to your platform APIs.

What we access

Our app connects to the Instagram Graph API and the TikTok Content Posting API using OAuth credentials tied to the @clutchsprint account only. Through these connections we:

  • Publish video content and captions to the connected @clutchsprint account
  • Read basic account status needed to confirm a publish succeeded

We do not access, collect, or store data belonging to any other account, follower, or viewer. We do not request permissions beyond what's needed to publish content.

What we store

We keep a local, private log of topics we've already covered (to avoid duplicate posts) and basic publishing history (what was posted, when, and to which platform). This data is not shared, sold, or used for any purpose beyond running this pipeline.

Third-party services

Our pipeline uses third-party APIs (Google Trends, YouTube Data API, Reddit API) to find trending sports topics, and an LLM provider to draft scripts. These calls send only public trend keywords and generated scripts — never platform user data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach out via the @clutchsprint account on Instagram or TikTok.
