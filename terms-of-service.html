ClutchSprint — Terms of Service

Last updated: August 18, 2026

These terms cover the automated publishing tool used by the @clutchsprint account on Instagram and TikTok.

What this tool does

ClutchSprint's pipeline detects trending sports topics, drafts a short video (script, voiceover, visuals), and — after review — publishes it to the @clutchsprint account via the Instagram Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API. It only ever posts to the @clutchsprint account itself; it does not act on behalf of any other user or account.

Acceptable use

This tool is used solely to manage content for @clutchsprint. It does not:

  • Post on behalf of third parties
  • Follow, like, comment, or message on other accounts
  • Scrape or store data about other users, followers, or viewers

Availability

This is a small, independently run project. We don't guarantee uptime or that every scheduled post will publish successfully — a human reviews output before it goes live.

Changes

We may update these terms as the tool evolves. The most current version is always at this URL.

Contact

Questions: reach out via the @clutchsprint account on Instagram or TikTok.
